Common questions

  • How do I scan my pantry? Open the Pantry section and tap "Snap or add photos." The app will identify what it can see and add it to your inventory.
  • How do I scan a receipt? In the Pantry section, tap "Snap or add receipt photo." Review the items it found before confirming.
  • Is everything in the app really kosher? The built-in recipe library follows Chabad standards (Cholov Yisroel dairy, Chabad-approved shechita for meat). Community-submitted recipes are automatically reviewed and relabeled to the same standard before anyone can see them.
  • How do I share my plan with family? Go to Family Account and create a household code, then have your family members join with that code on their own devices.
  • My data isn't syncing. Make sure you're connected to the internet and that you and your family are using the same household code. Email us if it's still not working.

Privacy

We only collect what's needed to run the app - your household's plan, pantry, and shopping data (synced only with your own family), and a name/email if you sign in to share a community recipe. We never sell your data.
